# Project video provenance

`brag.mp4` is a 16.5-second silent H.264 montage, assembled September 30, 2026 from four captioned slides. The live dashboard images on slides 1 and 2 are a saved browser capture from the verified Render session on September 29, 2026 (`/downloads/cloud-browser-20260929-102603.png` in the authoring workspace). Slide 3 uses the repository's previously labeled synthetic `docs/demo-dashboard.png`. Slide 4 gives architecture, release test count, and free-tier limits. The video does not contain a continuous live market recording and does not establish persistent uptime or investment performance. Its numbers are from one simulated session, not actual returns.

The PNG slides in `frames/` and `brag-poster.png` are editable source and poster. Neither control secrets nor private account information appear in the captures. Render's free database expiry was shown as October 29, 2026 on its own dashboard at deployment.
